Privacy policy

OrbitBills by TechSerenia is a local-first billing app. Business data (products, clients, invoices, stock, settings) is stored in your browser’s IndexedDB on this device only. It is not uploaded to TechSerenia servers.

  • No cloud account is required for day-to-day use.
  • Clearing site data in the browser removes local data — use Admin → Backup before clearing.
  • Optional contact form messages on the marketing site may be sent by email if you choose to write to us.
