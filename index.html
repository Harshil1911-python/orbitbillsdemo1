Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can I import my existing clients and invoices?

Yes. Techserenia accepts CSV imports for clients, plans, and historical invoices during setup.

Is my billing data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is controlled per team member role.

Can I customize invoices with my own branding?

Yes. Add your logo, brand colors, and custom notes to every invoice before it's sent.

Do you support recurring and one-time invoices together?

Both live in the same dashboard, so you can manage subscriptions and one-off bills side by side.

What happens if a client's payment fails?

You're notified instantly, and Techserenia can automatically retry or send a follow-up reminder.

Can I cancel or change my plan anytime?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings.
