About OrbitBills

Billing software, built by people tired of chasing invoices.

Techserenia builds OrbitBills, the billing engine behind this page, because we got tired of watching teams run their invoicing out of spreadsheets, email threads, and sticky notes.

Why we started

Most billing tools are either too simple to trust with real revenue, or too complex for a small team to set up in an afternoon. We wanted something in between: clear enough to hand to anyone on the team, and solid enough to run recurring billing on.

OrbitBills started as an internal tool to solve that exact problem, then became the product this page is showing you. Every feature on this site exists because a real invoice needed it first.

How we build

We keep the product deliberately calm: one dashboard, clear statuses, no dashboards-within-dashboards. When something is overdue, you should be able to see it in one glance, not dig for it.

"We didn't want another tool that needed its own onboarding course. Billing should be the boring, reliable part of running a business."
The Techserenia team
What we believe

The principles behind OrbitBills.

These shape every feature we ship, from the invoice editor to the payment reminders.

Clarity first

Every screen should answer "what do I need to do next" without extra clicks or explanations.

Reliability over features

We'd rather ship fewer features that never fail than a long list that sometimes breaks.

Respect for your data

Your invoices and client details are yours. We treat access and encryption as a default, not an add-on.
